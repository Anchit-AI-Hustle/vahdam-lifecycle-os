How avatars fit the rest of the OS. Cohorts (in the Cohorts feature) are the raw lenses: RFM segments, engagement cohorts, lifecycle stages, product cohorts. An Avatar is a curated composite of those lenses plus market truth, given a name and a face. Analytics assigns a live customer to cohorts; the avatar tells the creative team what that person wants, fears and responds to. The market study underneath these avatars lives at docs/market-intelligence/us-coffee-d2c-landscape.md; the live profile fields are captured by schemas/cohort-profile.json; the automated actions are wired in config/retention-triggers.yaml.

The Four Avatars V2 - active

Four behavioural buyer profiles that drive VAHDAM Ashwagandha Coffee and wellness-tea growth. Each maps to specific cohorts, drawn verbatim from the platform Cohorts dictionary (Engagement Cohorts A to F, Lifecycle Stages, RFM Segments, Product and Behavioral cohorts), and carries hard planning numbers so a brief can be written against a person, not a percentage.

One customer can resolve to different avatars over their lifetime as their cohorts change. Always target by the avatar a campaign is written for, and keep the name verbatim across mailer, ad, landing-page and social briefs. Sources: blended analyst-style estimates (Euromonitor / IBISWorld-style ranges, public brand disclosures, and D2C benchmark reports) for internal planning.
