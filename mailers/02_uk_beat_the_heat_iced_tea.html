There is a kind of thirst that only a cold, clean glass of tea can settle. Our green teas cold steep beautifully: crisp, gently minty, and quietly refreshing, with none of the bitterness a rushed brew can bring.

No kettle. No fuss. Just a jug, the fridge, and a little patience.

Cold steep in three steps
1.  Drop four tea bags into a one litre jug of cold water.
2.  Rest it in the fridge overnight, or for at least six hours.
3.  Pour over ice, add a wedge of lemon or a sprig of mint, and steep the moment.
