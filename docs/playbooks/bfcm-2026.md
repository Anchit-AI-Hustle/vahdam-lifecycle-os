## 1. The central tension, stated first

BFCM is the year's largest **discount** event. VAHDAM's standing rule is that discounts are
**not a default lever**:

> "Straight discounts are reserved for **The Curious Switcher** and the **discount-responsive**
> behavioral cohort (and the coldest reactivation cases…). Everyone else gets value framing,
> not markdowns."
> — `knowledge/brand/04-offers-and-mechanics.md`, *Discount discipline*

These two facts are in direct conflict, and resolving it is the whole strategic problem. The
repo's own knowledge library already contains the sharpest framing of the risk:

> "Brands often lose money in Cyber Week by swapping full-margin purchases for discounted ones
> — model it before celebrating topline."
> — Drew Sanocki, `knowledge/retention/04-drew-sanocki.md`

**So the governing question for BFCM 2026 is not "what discount?" but "which cohorts would have
bought anyway?"** A record topline built by discounting Champions and Loyal is a margin
transfer, not growth. The cohort split in §3 is built to answer exactly that.

## 3. Cohort plan — who gets a price cut and who does not

Built from the cohort dictionary and the offer-selection guide, applying the discount rule
rather than overriding it for the season.

| Cohort | BFCM treatment | Mechanic | Discount? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Champions / Loyal / VIP / UK Cohort F** | **Early access**, 24–48h before public | Recognition + first pick of gifting range | **No.** Early access *is* the reward |
| **Coffee & supplement repeaters** | Stock-up moment | **B2G1 on the 3-Pack** — the standing mechanic, not a BFCM markdown | No (value, not markdown) |
| **Subscribers (Loop)** | Loyalty acknowledgement | Subscriber-only gift or bonus tin; never a price cut that undercuts their standing rate | **No** |
| **Gifting Connector** | Peak season, peak relevance | Advent Calendar, Signature Private Reserve, curated sets, starter-kit-as-gift (~$48 US / ~£40 UK gift value) | No — lead with the gift value already built in |
| **Curious Switcher / discount-responsive** | **This is who BFCM is for** | Sampler / starter entry at a genuine BFCM price | **Yes** |
| **At-Risk / Can-not-Lose-Them / UK Cohort A** | Win-back, strongest reason to return | Justified incentive | **Yes** |
| **Need-Attention / Hibernating** | Reactivation | Incentive scaled to how cold they are | **Yes, graded** |

**Why subscribers get no discount:** a public price below a subscriber's recurring rate teaches
them to cancel and re-buy on promotion. Loop is **7.6% of US revenue at a $39.83 AOV**
(`knowledge/brand/04-offers-and-mechanics.md`) — small enough to be fragile, valuable enough to
protect.

## 4. Send plan — and why it is shorter than most BFCM calendars

The frequency cap is **2 promotional sends per rolling 7 days, absolute maximum 3**
(`docs/campaign-orchestration-master-spec.md`). BFCM week does **not** suspend it. Most
published BFCM calendars run 8–14 sends across the week; that is incompatible with this cap
and with the brand's positioning.

The cap is also not merely a brand rule — it is a deliverability rule. From
`knowledge/retention/03-jimmy-kim.md`, the practitioner argument is that frequency *improves*
deliverability only when engagement holds; blasting an unsegmented file into peak week is how
brands land in Promotions for December.

**Proposed cadence (per cohort, within cap):**

| Date | Send | Audience | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wed 25 Nov | Early access opens | Champions, Loyal, VIP, Cohort F | founder-note / editorial |
| Fri 27 Nov | Black Friday | Public, segmented by cohort table above | `limited-drop-countdown` |
| Sun 29 Nov | Gifting reminder | Gifting Connector + non-openers of BF | `gift-bundle-showcase` |
| Mon 30 Nov | Cyber Monday | Public, grid-led | `product-grid-conversion` |
| Tue 1 Dec | **Grace window** close | BF/CM non-purchasers only | single-product-spotlight |

Any cohort receives at most **2** of these in a rolling 7-day window; the segmentation is what
makes that possible, not a relaxation of the rule.

**The grace window is deliberate.** From `knowledge/retention/08-ari-murray.md`:

> "Leave discount codes active briefly after a sale ends (Cyber Monday tip) — capturing
> stragglers costs nothing and prevents the worst post-purchase feeling (missing the deal by
> hours)."

Framed as a quiet extension to people who did not buy, never announced as an extension to
everyone — otherwise it trains lateness.

## 5. Coffee flows — the one place BFCM and the brand's economics agree

Ashwagandha Coffee is the strongest BFCM asset VAHDAM has, because its mechanics are
**already** value-based and need no seasonal markdown:

- **Subscription-first** is the standing frame for coffee and supplements
  (`knowledge/brand/04-offers-and-mechanics.md`). BFCM is a subscriber-acquisition moment, not
  a discount moment.
- **Starter Kit** is the documented on-ramp, and already carries a **~$48 (US) / ~£40 (UK)**
  gift. That is the BFCM offer — no price cut required.
- **B2G1 on the 3-Pack** rewards stock-up without discounting the single unit. This is exactly
  what a stock-up holiday wants.
- **Refill SKU** leads for repeat buyers, framed on rhythm ("before you run out"), never false
  urgency.

**Existing coffee assets in the repo to reuse rather than rebuild:**

| Asset | Path |
|---|---|
| Coffee upsell mailer (engagers) | `mailers/01_coffee_upsell_engagers.html` |
| Coffee launch → tea lovers (Cohort B), v1 + v2 | `lifecycle-campaigns/2026-07-03_week1/emails/` |
| Story-intro coffee (Cohort A), v1 + v2 | `lifecycle-campaigns/2026-07-03_week1/emails/` |
| UK Ashwagandha Coffee starter kit campaign | `lifecycle-campaigns/2026-07-09_ashwagandha-coffee-uk-starter-kit/` |
| Ashwagandha coffee landing pages (agent / no-agent) | `ashwagandha-coffee-landing-*.html` |
| Reels/stories video 1080×1920 + preview GIF | repo root, `assets/media/` |
| US coffee D2C competitive landscape | `docs/market-intelligence/us-coffee-d2c-landscape.md` |

The subscription-first framing means the coffee LTV case is the one place where a BFCM
acquisition at thin margin is defensible — a first coffee order that enters the Loop rhythm is
not a margin swap. `[DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: coffee subscription retention curve / payback
period]` — without it, "defensible" is a hypothesis, not a finding.

## 6. Practitioner guidance already captured in this repo

These are not my recommendations; they are what the retention library already holds, filtered
to BFCM. Sources and verification status are in each file.

- **Pre-mortem the margin swap** (`04-drew-sanocki.md`) — model how much peak-week revenue is
  demand you would have captured at full price. Also: front-load paid spend into the *cheaper
  discovery weeks before* BFCM and lean on owned channels during peak week.
- **Merchandising is retention** (`08-ari-murray.md`) — before the promo, restructure
  home/collection hierarchy around the offer. Her "Website North Stars: 11 sites that promoted
  BFCM perfectly" is named as the reference to revisit before every peak season.
- **The post-sale grace window** (`08-ari-murray.md`) — see §4.
- **Segment on sentiment, not just purchase history** (`06-joanne-coffey.md`) — five-star and
  one-star reviewers get different messaging; conditional content blocks render one campaign
  many ways. Her team's BFCM was **+150% YoY**, cited by Klaviyo on an earnings call — the
  strongest third-party verification available for a private brand.
- **Zero-party data beats inference** (`06-joanne-coffey.md`) — quiz completers had a **20%
  higher placed-order rate** than a standard welcome flow (published by Klaviyo).
- **Churn-risk save flows in plain text** (`06-joanne-coffey.md`) — for At-Risk cohorts, a
  plain-text note asking what went wrong, not a louder promo.
- **Retention subsidises acquisition** (`05-cody-plofker.md`, `04-drew-sanocki.md`) — the reason
  the cohort split in §3 protects best customers instead of harvesting them.

## 7. Blockers — what must be true before any of this can be generated

These are live as of 2026-08-20, measured from `/api/connectors-health` in production:

1. **`LIVE_CONNECTORS=on` is not set.** The catalog falls back to a static build artifact and
   `creative_blocked: true` — the gate refuses to generate any campaign. Klaviyo's API key is
   already configured and blocked only by this switch.
2. **The planner emits product names that do not exist in the catalog** (e.g. "Earl Grey Citrus
   Black Tea", "Sleep Herbal Tea — Lavender Chamomile"). Every such name resolves `weak` /
   `ambiguous`, which the catalog gate correctly refuses. Until generation is constrained to
   real catalog rows, no BFCM asset can pass the gate even with connectors live.
3. **No send path exists.** Klaviyo integration is read-only (12 `get_*` ops, no campaign
   create/send); social push is `not_integrated_phase_2`. Assets must be hand-carried into the
   ESP.
4. `[DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: BFCM 2025 baseline — revenue, AOV, discount rate, margin, and
   the discount-vs-full-price split by cohort]`. Without last year's numbers the §1 margin-swap
   pre-mortem cannot be run, only described.
5. `[DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: current free-shipping threshold per market]` — the offers
   playbook explicitly says to confirm this against the live store before quoting it.

**Lead time:** 99 days from 2026-08-20 to Black Friday. Items 1 and 2 are the critical path;
neither is a content problem.
