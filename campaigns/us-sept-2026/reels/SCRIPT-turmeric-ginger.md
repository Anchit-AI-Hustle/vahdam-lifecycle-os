## Script

Spoken lines and on-screen text are separated deliberately. They are not the same
copy, and reading one over the other is how a reel ends up saying everything twice.

| In | SPOKEN (VO or creator, to camera) | ON-SCREEN TEXT (burned, sound-off legible) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0s | "I gave up tea after three o'clock." | `I gave up tea after three o'clock.` |
| 1.5s | "Then I found one that has no caffeine in it at all." | `No caffeine. At all.` |
| 4.0s | "Turmeric chunks and ginger, steeped whole." | `CAFFEINE FREE · CERTIFIED ORGANIC` |
| 7.0s | "I have it at nine at night now." | — |
| 9.5s | "Same cup, none of the trade-off." | `100 infusion bags` |
| 12.0s | "Turmeric Ginger, twenty-five ninety-nine." | `Turmeric Ginger Herbal Tea, 100 Count` / `$25.99` / `SHOP NOW` |

**On-screen text at 4.0s is quoted from the box.** Every factual line in this script
is either the speaker's own experience or printed on the pack. There is no health
claim anywhere: "I have it at nine at night now" is an account of behaviour, not a
claim that the product causes sleep. That distinction is the whole reason the line is
phrased that way, and it must survive the edit.

## Crop plan

Shot 9:16 as the master. Everything critical stays inside the 1080x1920 safe box
(y=200 to y=1400, 120px clear right), which means both derived crops are lossless
re-frames rather than re-edits.

| Output | Use | Derivation |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 1080x1920 | TikTok, Reels, Stories | Master |
| 1:1 1080x1080 | Meta feed | Centre crop of the master. Type re-set, not scaled: the 104px headline becomes 84px. |
| 16:9 1920x1080 | YouTube | Centre band. Shots 2 and 3 are macro and survive it; shots 1 and 4 need a wider take, so **shoot them twice**, once framed for 9:16 and once for 16:9. |

A 16:9 crop of a 9:16 master loses 68% of the frame. Planning for it at the shoot
costs one extra setup; discovering it in the edit costs a reshoot.
