# Creator brief — Turmeric Ginger, US

**Product:** Turmeric Ginger Herbal Tea Tisane, 100 Count — $25.99
**Market:** United States only
**Placements:** TikTok in-feed, Instagram Reels
**This is the test arm.** A designed poster runs beside it as the control. See
`docs/test-plan.md`.

---

## The hook, in your own words

Open on the problem, not the product. Do not say the brand name, show the box, or
mention a price in the **first 1.5 seconds**. Your face or your hands, and a flat
statement of something true about your afternoon.

**Three alternates — pick the one that is actually true for you, or write a fourth:**

1. "I stopped drinking tea after three o'clock, because I like sleeping."
2. "Everything I want to drink at 9pm has caffeine in it."
3. "This is the only thing in my kitchen I can drink at night and still sleep."

If none of those is true for you, say so and we will rewrite. A hook you do not mean
is visible, and it is the frame the whole test is measured on.

## Three beats

**1. The problem (0 - 1.5s).** The line above. Nothing else in frame.

**2. The proof (1.5 - 12s).** Open the pyramid bag on camera. Show the turmeric
chunks and the ginger. Pour, and let the colour go gold. Say what it is in your own
words. This is where the box may first appear.

**3. The ask (12 - 20s).** Product, price, one call to action: **Shop Now**.

Total: 15 - 34s for TikTok, 15 - 30s for Reels.

## What must be on camera

- The **US box**, front facing, legible. `NET WT. 7.05 OZ (200g) • 100 INFUSION BAGS`.
- The **dry leaf** out of the bag. Turmeric chunks, visible.
- **You**, at least once. The account has run cold traffic without a person in frame
  and it did not work.

## What you may say

Anything true about your own experience, in your own voice. Plus these, which are
printed on the box:

- Caffeine free
- Certified organic / USDA Organic
- Non-GMO Project Verified
- Tastes great hot or iced
- 100 infusion bags, pyramid bags

## What you may NOT claim

This is a tea, not a supplement, and US rules on that distinction are strict.

- **No health or medical claims of any kind.** Not "reduces inflammation", not "helps
  digestion", not "boosts immunity", not "anti-inflammatory", not "detox", not "good
  for joints". Not even softened ("I feel like it helps my..."). If a claim implies
  treating, curing, preventing or affecting a condition, it cannot be said.
- **No sleep claim about the product.** You may say you drink it at night and you
  sleep fine. You may not say it *helps* you sleep — that is a different assertion,
  and it is not on the label.
- **No ratings, review counts or "best seller" claims.** We have no approved figure
  to give you, so there is nothing for you to repeat.
- **No discount, sale or percentage off.** The price is $25.99. If that changes, we
  will re-brief you in writing.
- **No comparison to a named competitor.**
- **No caffeine claim beyond "caffeine free"**, which is the on-pack wording.
