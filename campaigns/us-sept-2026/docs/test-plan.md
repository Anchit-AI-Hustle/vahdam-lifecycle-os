# Prospecting test — designed poster vs creator

## The disagreement, stated plainly

The brief asks for campaigns. The measured evidence says the way this account has
been buying cold traffic does not work, and one part of this set is built to argue
with the brief rather than execute it.

**What is measured:**

- Top-of-funnel Meta ROAS across the BF/CM windows: **0.88x**. Every dollar in
  returned 88 cents.
- The "New Awareness" pair: **$756 spent, $0 returned.** Two designed posters, cold
  audience, no creator in either.
- Category leader teardown (Pique, US Meta Ad Library, active image ads, read
  **2026-08-22**): **100%** creator collaborations posted as "handle with Pique".
  **Zero** designed posters. **Zero** price, percentage or countdown burned onto an
  image. **One** CTA across the set: Shop Now.

**What that implies:** shipping another designed poster to a cold audience is the
thing that already produced $0 on $756. But "creators work" is inference from a
competitor's media buy, not from this account's data. Nobody here has actually tested
it, so asserting it would be the same mistake in the other direction.

## The test

Two arms, one variable, one product, one price, one CTA.

| | Arm A — CONTROL | Arm B — TEST |
|---|---|---|
| Asset | `ads/meta-1080x1350-turmeric-ginger-CONTROL.png` | Creator footage, briefed in `creator/turmeric-ginger-creator-brief.md` |
| Form | Designed poster, brand-made | Person to camera, creator-made, creator VO |
| Product | Turmeric Ginger 100 Count | Same |
| Price | $25.99 | Same |
| CTA | Shop Now | Same |
| Audience | Cold prospecting | Same |
| Placement | Meta feed | Meta feed |

The static TikTok and Instagram cuts are **not part of the test** and should not be
mixed into it. They are supporting placements. Adding them splits the read.

## Why the control ships at all

Because deleting it does not settle anything. If only the creator arm runs and it
works, the account learns that creator content works at some ROAS — not that it beats
the thing it replaced. The control is what makes the $756 already spent readable
instead of merely regrettable.

If the control loses again, that is two independent results pointing the same way, and
the designed-poster format should come off cold prospecting entirely.

## Reading it

`[decision metric: confirm — ROAS, CAC or first-purchase rate]`
`[spend per arm and minimum runtime: confirm with media]`
`[significance threshold: confirm]`

Those three are bracketed on purpose. They are media-planning decisions with money
attached, and inventing a "$X per arm over Y days" here would be exactly the kind of
confident fabricated number this whole campaign is built to avoid.

What can be said without them: both arms need equal spend, the same audience
definition, and the same runtime, or the comparison is not a comparison.

## Inventory ceiling on this test

Turmeric Ginger 100 Count holds **1,913 units** at $25.99, so the SKU can absorb real
spend. That is why it is the hero and the $9.99 30-count is not: the 30-count is the
better cold-traffic price but holds **280 units**, roughly $2,800 of sellable stock,
which a prospecting campaign would clear in days and then keep spending against an
out-of-stock PDP.

Put the 30-count on the landing page as a trade-down instead.
