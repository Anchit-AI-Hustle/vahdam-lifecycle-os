# Shot briefs — the photography this campaign does not have

## Why this document exists

The composition rule asks for three layers in every visual: a graded **scene**
photograph, the **brand** layer, and the **catalog** layer. This campaign has two of
the three.

The store supplies pack shots on white. It supplies **no lifestyle photography** —
no hands, no kitchens, no pours, no people. So the scene layer was not built, and it
was not faked either:

- **It was not AI-generated.** Allowed for scene and atmosphere with disclosure, but
  a generated kitchen behind a real pack invites a generated pack next, and the
  packaging carries regulated label copy. The line is easier to hold at "no".
- **It was not borrowed from another market.** A UK or EU lifestyle frame would drag
  a UK or EU pack into shot.
- **The ground is honestly designed instead** — brand-toned radial fields with film
  grain and a vignette. Real design, not a photograph pretending to be one.

So the six static ads and three storyboard frames are **layout-locked and
photography-pending**. Type, price, brand and pack are final and verified. The ground
is a placeholder with a plan. Commission the four shots below and they drop straight
in behind the existing type without a re-layout.

## SHOT 01 — "The four o'clock counter" (hero, Turmeric Ginger)

**Feeds:** `meta-1080x1350-turmeric-ginger-CONTROL`, reel beat 1 (HOOK)

- **Frame:** 4:5 and 9:16 masters, shot 9:16 and cropped up.
- **Subject:** hands only, mid-thirties to mid-fifties, no face. A plain ceramic mug
  set down on a wooden or stone kitchen counter.
- **Light:** late afternoon, low and raking from camera left. Long shadow across the
  counter. This is the whole point of the shot: the time of day IS the message.
- **Copy space:** upper 55% of the 4:5 frame must stay quiet enough to hold a
  three-line serif headline in cream. Keep the counter line low.
- **Must be in frame:** steam, or condensation, or the visible act of setting down.
- **Must NOT be in frame:** the pack, the logo, any type, any brand cue. The hook
  rule forbids all of them before 1.5s, and this plate is the first frame.
- **Grade:** brand-toned scrim, 7-16% grain, 1.1x-1.8x crop.

## SHOT 02 — "Dry leaf and pyramid bag" (proof, Turmeric Ginger)

**Feeds:** reel beat 2 (PROOF), Instagram editorial alternate treatment

- **Frame:** square and 9:16.
- **Subject:** macro. An opened pyramid bag, turmeric chunks and ginger pieces spilled
  on a cream surface. Then the pour: water hitting the leaf, the colour going gold.
- **Must be in frame:** the actual chunk texture. The pack says "whole turmeric
  chunks"; the shot has to earn that word or the copy is doing it alone.
- **Must NOT be in frame:** a competitor's tea bag shape; loose dust that reads as
  low grade.

## SHOT 03 — "The morning tin" (hero, Daily Assam)

**Feeds:** `meta-1080x1350-daily-assam-RETARGETING`, email hero block

- **Frame:** 4:5.
- **Subject:** the 12 oz box open on a counter with a scoop of leaf, mug half made,
  milk about to go in. Morning light, cool and flat, deliberately the opposite of
  Shot 01's low sun.
- **Reason it must differ:** this campaign already uses the Assam pack on an ink
  ground. Reusing one photo at one treatment across two placements breaks the
  no-repeat rule. Different time of day, different grade.
- **Copy space:** left third clear, headline sets left.

## SHOT 04 — "Twenty-four mornings" (hero, Advent)

**Feeds:** `story-1080x1920-advent-HOLIDAY`, `youtube-1920x1080-advent-ENDCARD`

- **Frame:** 9:16 and 16:9.
- **Subject:** the foldable calendar hanging, one pocket opened, a hand taking out
  day 01. Domestic December setting: a door, a shelf edge, warm lamp not daylight.
- **Must be in frame:** the numbered pockets legible enough to read as a calendar.
- **Must NOT be in frame:** any date, any countdown, any price burned into the scene.
  The order-by date is unconfirmed (see `catalogue-verification.md`) and a date in a
  photograph cannot be corrected later without a reshoot.

## What is genuinely blocked, not just missing

**Video.** Everything in `reels/` is pre-production: storyboard frames, a shot list
and a script. There is no shot footage and therefore no reel. The frames are stamped
`STORYBOARD · NOT FINAL` in-image so a frame cannot be mistaken for a delivered asset
if it is pulled out of this folder on its own.

**Creator content.** The creator brief in `creator/` is ready to send. It cannot be
produced in-house by definition, and the whole point of the test is that the footage
comes from a person who is not this brand.
