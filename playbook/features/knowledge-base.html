Buyer cohorts

The four audiences

The demographic and behavioural segments every brief targets. Product avatars are the persuasion layer that speaks to these buyers.

Third-Wave Purists

Loyalist
Income band
$95k to $180k
Roast preference
Light to medium, single-origin

Buys on provenance and craft. Reads taste notes and estate names. Full-margin, protect from a discount habit. Hook: origin story and cup character.

Functional Optimizers

Subscriber
Income band
$70k to $140k
Roast preference
Adaptogen blend, medium

Comes for the functional payload and the routine. Highest subscription bias. Hook: refill rhythm, Ashwagandha Coffee as a daily ritual.

Biohacking Performers

High-intent
Income band
$110k to $220k
Roast preference
Medium-dark, high-dose adaptogen

Optimises inputs and tracks outcomes. Responds to dose, sourcing, and stacking logic. Hook: precise payload and companion supplements.

Routine Convenience

Switcher
Income band
$55k to $110k
Roast preference
Medium, ground or instant

Buys for ease and price sensitivity. The discount-responsive switcher. Hook: convenience proof, low-friction reorder, second-order nudge.
