The store, rebuilt as a 3D scene

A true-to-brand replica of Vahdam's digital signature rendered as a continuous WebGL scene: floating product panels, glassmorphic surfaces, brand-cloned colours and typography. Live catalog and pricing come from the regional Shopify storefront; historical metrics come from the Snowflake → Supabase daily mirror. On low-end / mobile / reduced-motion devices it degrades automatically to a fast 2D brand layout so ad-traffic conversion is never sacrificed.

Powered by Vahdam3DConnectorEngine. On the bundled React app it mounts as a context provider (marketing_automation/src/Vahdam3DConnectorEngine.tsx); here on the static site it loads through the no-build ESM bridge (/assets/vahdam3d-bridge.js). Meta-ads landers (try.vahdam.com, try.vahdam.co.uk) auto-collapse the scene into a single-product spatial checkout.
