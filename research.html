Positioning

VAHDAM is premium, single-estate Indian heritage: tea, Ashwagandha Coffee, and functional supplements, shipped garden-fresh from origin. The brand competes on provenance and a real functional payload, not price. B-Corp and ethical sourcing are proof points, not the headline.

Voice

Warm, sensory, emotionally resonant, story-driven. Testimonials read as tiny personal stories, not reviews. Sample register: There is a moment when the right cup of tea does more than warm your hands.

Palette (only four)

Forest green #004A2B · Gold #AB8743 · Near-black #171717 · Cream #FBF5EA. On green, text is only cream or gold.

Typography

Headings: Lao MN (fallback Cormorant Garamond, Georgia, serif). Body: Proxima Nova (fallback Helvetica Neue, Arial). No other fonts in emailers.

Preferred lexicon

ritual, restore, balance, origin, single-estate, hand-picked, steep, heritage, crafted.

Banned

wellness journey, transform, liquid gold, game-changer, LIMITED TIME (caps), hurry, last chance, while supplies last. No em/en dashes in copy.

Competitor-mailer capture flow

Competitor sends email→ Cloudflare Email Worker→ FastAPI /v1/incoming-mail→ Parse headers + extract HTML→ Postgres competitor_mailers + S3 snapshot→ CompetitorMailViewer (React)
  • Cloudflare Email Worker — receives inbound mail to the capture address and forwards the raw SMTP payload to the engine.
  • FastAPI capture endpoint — /v1/incoming-mail parses headers, extracts the high-fidelity HTML body, and archives it.
  • Postgres — competitor_mailers (sender, subject, received_at, extracted_html_body, s3_snapshot_url) indexed by sender and received-at.
  • Viewer — a React viewer browses captured competitor mailers for the benchmarking workflow.

Inside the OS this powers Competitor Benchmarking; the owned-store side powers Data Analysis. Repo: vahdam_dtc_data_engine. Source detail: knowledge/brand/06-market-intelligence-summary.md.
