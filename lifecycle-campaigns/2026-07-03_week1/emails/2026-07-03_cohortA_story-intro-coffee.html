There is an hour the day hasn’t claimed yet.
 
We make it something to look forward to.

It arrives before the emails do. The kettle ticking as it cools. A window still holding the early light. The small ceremony of choosing a cup. For a few unhurried minutes, the morning belongs entirely to you.

We have spent years inside that moment — steeping teas from Assam and the high Himalayas, learning that the way a day begins quietly shapes everything after it. Now we have made a coffee for it: blended with ashwagandha, a root woven through Indian heritage, for a gentler kind of energy.

It is meant to be taken slowly. The warmth of the cup in both hands, the steam rising, the first sip that asks nothing of you. Not a jolt — a beginning.
