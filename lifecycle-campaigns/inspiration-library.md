# Email Inspiration Library — Visual Formatting Patterns

**What this is:** A visual-formatting knowledge base extracted from 33 screenshots of marketing emails (captured from milled.com, ~3 emails per screenshot, ~99 emails total) from DTC coffee-alternative / supplement / tea brands: RYZE Superfoods, AG1, Space Goods, Grüns, Teabox, and others. The focus is LAYOUT MECHANICS — hero treatments, section order, CTA styling, product presentation, promo devices, palette and typography habits — so future VAHDAM lifecycle emails can borrow proven visual structures while staying inside VAHDAM's brand gates.

**Source:** Google Drive screenshots dated 2026-07-03. Scanned: 2026-07-03.

**How to use:** Jump to "Formatting playbook for VAHDAM" at the bottom for the synthesized reusable patterns. Per-screenshot notes below are the raw evidence.

## 01 — Screenshot 1.36.00 PM (id 1wPw-a2hvyJZYNbghghR2ExnRfIOPK5kU)
Milled grid view, 4 email previews (top fold only).

**Email 1 — RYZE Superfoods (US, Jul 2) — 4th of July sale.**
- Hero: stacked promo system, not a single hero. Top utility bar (cream bg, 3 columns: "SHOP 42% OFF | RYZE | COMMUNITY" in rust/brown caps) → slim festive banner (sky-blue bg, navy "4TH OF JULY SALE" + mascot illustration + US flag + "UP TO 42% OFF + FREE Gifts With Any Bundle" + small navy SHOP NOW pill) → giant typographic headline on cream: "BIG WEEKENDS" (rust serif-ish display, ~2x body) over "REQUIRE BIG ENERGY" (dark brown, letterspaced).
- CTA: full-width-ish rounded rectangle, rust/terracotta fill, dark outline + hard drop shadow (retro sticker feel), white caps "SAVE UP TO 42%". Sits directly under 2 lines of supporting copy.
- Below CTA: full-width lifestyle photo (fireworks + whipped drink) as a visual reward strip.
- Palette: cream bg, rust/terracotta, navy, sky blue. Type: bold condensed display + clean sans body. Image:text ~50:50.
- Steal: the "offer bar above the hero" pattern — headline stays emotional while the discount lives in a slim top banner.

**Email 2 — Space Goods (GB, Jul 2) — Summer sale "90-day reset".**
- Hero: white header with small wordmark, then a full-bleed magenta/purple product photo block: two foil pouches on a vivid gradient. Slim caps micro-banner INSIDE the image top edge: "SUMMER SALE: BUY 2, GET 1 FREE".
- Price device: pink circle badge with strikethrough "£69" above "£58" — price-drop sticker directly on the hero photo.
- Offer device #2: black rectangular tag lower-left "BUY 2, GET 1 FREE" (white caps) — looks like a label stuck on the photo.
- Type on image: huge white sans "YOUR 90-DAY RESET" left-aligned over the gradient.
- Below: plain black-on-white body copy paragraphs (editorial, no decoration). Image:text ~60:40.
- Steal: strikethrough-price circle badge + offer tag layered directly onto hero photography; single loud accent color (magenta) against neutral email chrome.

**Emails 3 & 4 — AG1 (US, Jul 1) — Travel Kit GWP (two variants, same layout).**
- Hero: typographic-first. Warm off-white/cream bg, large forest-green SERIF headline ("Healthy habits shouldn't stop when vacation starts.") centered, 3–4 lines of small centered sans body beneath.
- Product: single hero object — green travel pouch photographed straight-on on seamless cream bg (product-cutout feel).
- Promo device: white starburst/scalloped sticker overlapping the product top-left: "FREE KIT with first subscription" in small caps — GWP called out as a badge, not a banner.
- Palette: cream + deep forest green only (2 colors!). Very close to VAHDAM's #FBF5EA/#004A2B. Serif headline + tiny sans support = high size contrast, calm and premium.
- Image:text ~50:50. No visible discount, no urgency — gift framing instead.
- Steal: AG1's serif-headline-on-cream + single-object product shot + scalloped "FREE GIFT" sticker is essentially a ready-made VAHDAM template.

## 02 — Screenshot 1.36.12 PM (id 1HAKLJheCq6tozHjt3zgzLbPyS9NHzzYJ)
Zoomed-in view of the same three emails as screenshot 01 (RYZE 4th-of-July / Space Goods 90-day reset / AG1 Travel Kit). Extra detail visible at this zoom:
- RYZE: the rust CTA has a subtle darker offset shadow (button reads "pressable"); top banner CTA "SHOP NOW" is a small rust rectangle inside the blue banner — two CTAs above the fold, one micro one macro.
- Space Goods: pouch photography is color-matched to the gradient bg (purple pouch on magenta) — monochrome saturation as a brand device; the strikethrough £69 uses a diagonal slash; "BUY 2, GET 1 FREE" tag is a white-bordered black chip.
- AG1: travel bag has a woven "Morning Person" label — merch-as-gift close-up; headline serif is high-contrast (Tiempos-like); body copy is ~13px sans, max ~60 chars/line, centered.
